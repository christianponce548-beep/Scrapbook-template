A LETTER FROM YOUR ST GEORGE CLASS

For our adviser.

Take your time. This one comes from all of us.

ST GEORGE · WITH GRATITUDEA YEAR WE’LL REMEMBER
SG
♥

Dear Adviser,

Somehow, a whole school year can fly by in a blink. Before it does, we wanted to pause and say what we should say more often: thank you.

Thank you for greeting us each day, guiding us when we weren’t sure of the way, and reminding us that we were capable of more than we thought. Your patience made room for our questions, your encouragement helped us try again, and your care made our classroom feel like a place where we belonged.

You gave us more than lessons. You gave us the confidence to grow, the courage to make mistakes and learn from them, and so many ordinary days that turned into memories we’ll keep. We’ll carry your advice and kindness with us long after this year is over.

We hope this little collection of moments reminds you how much you mean to us. We are grateful for every bit of time, heart, and faith you gave our class.

With all our appreciation,

Your St George class ♥

P.S. No matter how far we go, we’ll always be cheering for you, too.
